Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026.

This policy covers the private Data Connection Tools in GameForge, including the Google OAuth application currently named AdMob 数据连接. It covers reporting connections, not the separate games' privacy practices.

Data accessed and used

With your authorization, the tools access account, channel, video, or app identifiers and relevant metadata; aggregate reports about views, watch time, interactions, subscribers, advertising, revenue, or app performance; and connection status and synchronization records. OAuth tokens or other credentials are used to retrieve the reports you request. The tools do not ask for your Google password.

Data is used to synchronize and display reports, compare performance over time, and diagnose connection issues for the account owner and their authorized collaborators. Permissions are limited to those needed for the selected connection.

Storage and sharing

Reports are stored in the owner's private workspace database. Managed connection credentials are stored separately in files with restricted local permissions. Neither is published on this website. Requests to Google use HTTPS.

Data is available to the account owner and people or tools they explicitly authorize to assist with reporting. It is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Data is sent to the connected provider as needed to authenticate and retrieve reports. Any additional sharing requires the owner's authorization or a legal obligation.

The tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Reports are retained for the owner's reporting needs while the connection is in use. You can stop scheduled synchronization in the workspace and revoke Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops further authorized retrieval; it does not by itself erase previously imported reports.

To remove a connection's stored credentials and imported reports, use the workspace's permanent-delete option. You may also contact us below to request access, correction, or deletion. Copies you exported or backed up must be removed separately. The owner is responsible for reviewing retained history and removing data no longer needed, including after authorization is revoked.

Providers and this website

The tools use YouTube API Services and other explicitly connected providers. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

These information pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when data practices change. Material changes to access or use require appropriate notice and authorization. Send privacy questions or requests to ccaiassistant@gmail.com.
