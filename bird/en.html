Privacy Policy

Bird respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

1. Information Collection

The App itself does not directly collect personally identifiable information.

However, it uses third-party services such as Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • Usage data (ad impressions, clicks)

2. Use of Information

  • Provide personalized advertisements
  • Improve ad relevance and performance
  • Analyze ad performance

3. Tracking

The App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

  • If you allow tracking, IDFA may be used for personalized ads
  • If you deny, only non-personalized ads will be shown

You can change this anytime in:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google AdMob:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Some data may be processed by third-party providers for advertising purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us via the App.
