Data Connection Tools

Private tools for connecting accounts, synchronizing reports, and reviewing content and app performance in the GameForge workspace.

The Google OAuth application is currently displayed as AdMob 数据连接. This page covers its reporting connections, including YouTube and AdMob.

Purpose

The tools help their owner and explicitly authorized collaborators review their own accounts. They import performance reports on demand or on a configurable schedule and retain report history for comparison. Access to the workspace is private; this website provides information only.

Connected data

  • YouTube: channel and video identifiers, video metadata, and aggregate performance reports such as views, watch time, interactions, and subscriber changes.
  • AdMob and app stores: account and app identifiers and aggregate advertising, revenue, and app performance reports, when connected.

Google reporting connections request read-only access. They do not upload, edit, or delete YouTube videos, or post comments. Each connection requires the account owner's authorization.

Privacy and control

Reports and connection credentials are stored in the owner's private workspace, not on this public website. Read the Privacy Policy for data use, storage, deletion, and authorization controls.

Terms of use

Use the tools only with accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Reports may be delayed or revised by their providers. These tools use YouTube API Services; by using the YouTube connection, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Other connected services remain subject to their applicable terms.
